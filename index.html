Episode 3: Handling Tantrums Calmly (12 min)
🧘 Stay grounded ▾
Your calm regulates their storm. Take 3 deep breaths before speaking. Lower your voice pitch instead of raising it.
🤗 Name their feeling ▾
Help them name what they feel: "You are feeling disappointed right now." Naming an emotion tames its intensity.
🤝 Connect before correcting ▾
Offer a warm hug, gentle touch, or soft eye contact. Connection opens a child's receptive state before offering guidance.
