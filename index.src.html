Menjadi Teladan: Living What We Teach

📖 ကျမ်းချက် / Scripture
"Train up a child in the way he should go; even when he is old he will not depart from it."
"သူငယ်သွားအပ်သောလမ်း၌ သွန်သင်လေ့ကျင့်လော့။ သို့ပြုလျှင် သူသည်အသက်ကြီးသော်လည်း ထိုလမ်းမှမလွှဲမရှောင်ဘဲ နေလိမ့်မည်။"
— သုတ္တံကျမ်း ၂၂:၆ / Proverbs 22:6
Children may not always listen to what we say, but they never fail to imitate what we do. When our children see us handle frustration with calm patience, they learn emotional self-regulation. When they see us seek forgiveness after losing our temper, they learn humility.

Episode 3: Handling Tantrums Calmly (12 min)
🧘 Stay grounded ▾
Your calm regulates their storm. Take 3 deep breaths before speaking. Lower your voice pitch instead of raising it.
🤗 Name their feeling ▾
Help them name what they feel: "You are feeling disappointed right now." Naming an emotion tames its intensity.
🤝 Connect before correcting ▾
Offer a warm hug, gentle touch, or soft eye contact. Connection opens a child's receptive state before offering guidance.
